Earth

  • Radius: 6,371 km
  • Orbit: 67 km/sec
  • Moon: One
  • Rings: Zero

Earth is the third closest planet to the sun and our home. Earth is the only planet in our solar system which is habitable for life. Its atmosphere is made up of nitrogen and oxygen and 1% other gases. Liquid water is only fonud on earth and it covers about 71% of earth's surface. Earth is the only planet not named after a god, its name comes from the germanic word form "ground".
