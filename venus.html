Venus

  • Radius: 6,051 km
  • Orbit: 35 km/sec
  • Moon: Zero
  • Rings: Zero

Venus is the second closest planet to the sun and also the hotest. Dispite being further from the sun than mercury, its atmosphere is thick and traps heat. Tempertures on the surface are around 467C. Its atmosphere is mostly carbon dioxide, which is a key reson for its hot temperature. Venus gets its name from the roman goddess for love and beauty, which makes it the only planet named after a woman.
