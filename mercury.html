Mercury

  • Radius: 2,440 kilometers
  • Orbit: 47 kilometers/sec
  • Moon: Zero
  • Rings: Zero

Mercury is the closest planet to the sun and also the smallest. Mercury's temperature varies widely due to its distance from the sun. Temperture during the day can reach up to 430C, and as low as -180C during the night. Its exosphere made oxygen, sodium, hydrogen, helium, and potassium. Mercury gets its name from the roman god mercurius who is known for his swiftness.
